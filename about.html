Who We Are

Campus Music Club is a student-focused community that encourages musical creativity, teamwork, and confidence. We provide opportunities for students to learn, practise, collaborate, and perform.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring students together through music and provide a welcoming environment for beginners and experienced performers.

Club Activities

  • Weekly practice sessions
  • Music workshops
  • Student performances
  • Inter-college competitions
